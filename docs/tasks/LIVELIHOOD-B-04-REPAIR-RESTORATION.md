# LIVELIHOOD-B-04 — Repair and restoration

**Status:** roadmap placeholder; needs design and explicit approval  
**Parent:** Direction B livelihood roadmap  
**Prerequisite:** B-01 to B-03 proven playable; item condition rules inspected.

## Intended player outcome

The player restores damaged goods through a hands-on service activity, then keeps, returns or sells the restored physical item.

## Candidate scope

- One repairable item category and one authored repair station/tool.
- Condition-aware inputs and a clear before/after result.
- A small authored parts requirement.
- Optional customer contract integration only after B-02 proves the contract path.

## Design questions

- Does repair restore condition by fixed steps or to a defined target?
- Can quality/authenticity change? Recommendation: no; repair changes condition only.
- Are repairs performed on player-owned items only, or can a customer temporarily entrust an item?
- What happens if a repair attempt fails?
- Are tools consumed, durable or simply workspace prerequisites?

No repair rules or content are approved by this placeholder.
