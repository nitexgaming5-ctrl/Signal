# LIVELIHOOD-B-05 — Social life and recurring customers

**Status:** roadmap placeholder; needs design and explicit approval  
**Parent:** Direction B livelihood roadmap  
**Prerequisite:** B-02 single contract loop and current NPC relationship/knowledge foundations.

## Intended player outcome

Residents become recognisable customers and social connections. Repeated interactions can create access to information, requests and opportunities without reducing relationships to a single universal reputation score.

## Candidate scope

- A small authored set of customer characters.
- Player-driven conversations or favours with explicit outcomes.
- Recurring customer context and limited persistent relationship consequences.
- Information and introductions as possible rewards.

## Guardrails

- Preserve directed familiarity/trust/affinity rather than replacing them with one score.
- Ambient NPC routines must not create dramatic relationship changes by themselves.
- No automatic city-wide gossip or omniscient NPC knowledge.
- No relationship bonus to prices until separately designed.

No relationship changes, favour rules, customer preferences or recurring demand are approved by this placeholder.
