# LIVELIHOOD-B-07 — Neighbourhood expansion

**Status:** roadmap placeholder; needs design and explicit approval  
**Parent:** Direction B livelihood roadmap  
**Prerequisite:** first workshop, contracts and at least one social/customer loop are user-verified.

## Intended player outcome

A second compact area adds new residents, services, customers and opportunities that connect to the existing city rather than becoming an isolated content map.

## Candidate scope

- One additional compact district or connected block.
- A small cast of authored residents and businesses with overlapping routines.
- At least two reasons to revisit the area, such as a service, customer, supplier or social connection.
- Reuse the same player, GameSession, clock, inventory, account and NPC world.

## Guardrails

- Density before map size.
- No separate economy, NPC simulation or save context per district.
- New businesses and production sites are authored and event-driven.
- Do not expand geography until the existing neighbourhood has enough meaningful interactions to justify it.

No district layout, new cast or business content is approved by this placeholder.
